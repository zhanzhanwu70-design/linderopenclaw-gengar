Today's headline: Fight over fossil fuels leaves COP31 climate talks in deadlock.

Negotiations at the COP31 climate summit in Antalya, Turkey, are in deadlock and have been suspended for the night after running more than a day over time. Nearly two hundred countries cannot agree on a new global climate deal. The main sticking point is fossil fuels. The latest draft makes no reference to cutting demand for oil, gas and coal, after producers such as Saudi Arabia pushed for that language to be removed, and Panama, France, the Pacific islands and the EU are fighting to get it back in. A second dispute is money, with the Africa Group demanding more funding for adaptation. If there is no consensus, the summit would end with no deal and be reconvened next year. That has happened only once before since 1995.
