Today's top story: Five E U countries announce a migrant deportation centre in Rwanda. Germany, Denmark, Austria, Greece and the Netherlands say they will open a return hub next year, ready to accept migrants whose asylum claims have already been rejected. The plan starts as a small pilot of around one hundred people, and single men will go first. Once in Rwanda, people can either travel home or integrate into society, with access to work, education and healthcare. Human rights groups warn that this could violate migrant rights and help finance a government with a poor record of repression.
